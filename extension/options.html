Agent Inbox

Point this at your inbox and you can send any page with one click.

The web address of your inbox, e.g. https://agent-inbox.you.workers.dev

Open your inbox, go to Set up, and copy the key shown there.

Stored locally in this browser only — never synced to any account. Chrome will ask permission to talk to your inbox address; that's the extension asking for access to that one site, nothing else.
